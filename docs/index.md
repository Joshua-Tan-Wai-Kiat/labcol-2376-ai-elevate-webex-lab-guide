## Scenario

You will prepare a Webex Calling organization, connect it to premises-based PSTN service through a registration-based Local Gateway, and validate inbound and outbound calling. You will then enable Webex AI features for live captions, call transcripts, and recorded-call summaries.

The exercises use a dCloud pod, Workstation 1, Webex Control Hub, a Local Gateway, the Webex App, and PSTN test calls. Follow the pod-specific values displayed in your environment whenever they differ from examples in the guide.
